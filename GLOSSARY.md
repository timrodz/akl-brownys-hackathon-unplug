# EasyRe domain

Auckland residents describe household items they want to keep out of landfill; the app matches them to catalogue items and nearby businesses, then shows reduce, reuse, repair, recycle, and responsible disposal options.

## Language

**Submission**:
What a resident sends in through the form: description, location, and optionally a photo.
_Avoid_: Form, enquiry, ticket

**Item**:
A catalogue entry for a kind of unwanted material or product (for example lithium batteries, laptops, textiles), usually seeded from Auckland Council’s “where does it go?” directory or a material layer such as RecycleMap.
_Avoid_: Product, waste stream, SKU

**Submission item**:
The single catalogue item matched to one submission.
_Avoid_: Form item, line item

**Business**:
An organisation or place that can reuse, repair, collect, or accept drop-off for one or more items (Council directory org, RecycleMap marker, repair café, scheme drop-off).
_Avoid_: Organisation, facility, POI

**Business item**:
Which items a business accepts or services, and in what role (drop-off, collection, repair, reuse).
_Avoid_: Service offering, acceptance rule

**Recommendation**:
One persisted row in the 4R answer shown after a submission: pathway, short title and body, and optionally a linked business and distance.
_Avoid_: Result, option, tip

**Pathway**:
One of reduce, reuse, repair, recycle, or dispose — the order residents see when guidance is shown.
_Avoid_: R-strategy, hierarchy, action type
